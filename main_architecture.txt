Core Architecture Plan for main.ino1. Central Hardware & Bus Initialization (setup)Unified I²C Bus: Initialize the shared I²C bus once (Wire.begin(8, 9) at 400kHz) for both the SSD1306 OLED and the VL53L0X ToF sensor.Driver Instantiation: Initialize and verify all 6 modular subsystems:KeypadDriver: Non-blocking matrix scanner.ScaleDriver: HX711 with EWMA filtering and stability lock engine.LengthDriver: VL53L0X ToF with median filtering and clinical range bounds.UIEngine: Screen renderer and layout controller.SDDriver: Dual-folder manager (/pre_val/ cache and /post_val/ outbox).CloudClient: Wi-Fi manager and HTTP POST client.2. 5-State Bedside Clinical FSM (loop)State 1: STATE_PATIENT_ID (Registration)Captures alphanumeric Birth Certificate IDs from the keypad into a fixed buffer.* handles backspace; # validates length and transitions to Tare.Background Sync runs during this state.State 2: STATE_TARE_PROMPT (Zero Platform)Instructs clinician to clear the bed or place standard blankets.Pressing # executes scale.tare() and auto-transitions to Weighing.State 3: STATE_WEIGHING (Infant Mass Acquisition)Polls HX711 at 10Hz, applies EWMA filtering, and evaluates stability across a rolling 30-sample window.Locks when jitter $\le 8\text{g}$; pressing # freezes the measurement and moves to Length.State 4: STATE_MEASURING_LENGTH (Infant Stature Acquisition)Samples ToF at 5Hz, applies a 10-sample median filter, and validates WHO bounds (45.0 cm to 80.0 cm).Locks when jitter $\le 5\text{mm}$; pressing # confirms length and triggers the WHO diagnostic engine.State 5: STATE_DIAGNOSTIC_SUMMARY (Bedside Triage & Commit)Executes evaluatePatientDiagnostics() on-chip to determine WLZ Z-score and acute malnutrition status (SAM, MAM, NORMAL).Displays diagnostic results on the OLED.Pressing * resets the measurement pipeline (Redo).Pressing # commits the record via the dual-path routing engine.3. Dual-Path Save & Sync DispatcherIf Wi-Fi is Online at Bedside:Checks /pre_val/<ID>.csv to extract baseline Z-score $Z(t-1)$.Computes live centile drift $\Delta Z = Z(t) - Z(t-1)$ (FALTERING, CATCH_UP, STABLE, or BASELINE).Sends full JSON payload to Cloud API via HTTP POST.On HTTP 200/201, appends verified record to /pre_val/<ID>.csv and displays "Synced ☁️".If Offline (No Wi-Fi):Tags trajectory as "PENDING".Writes payload directly to /post_val/<ID>_<millis>.json.Verifies flash write and flashes "Saved Offline 💾" on the OLED.4. Silent Background Sync Worker (runSilentBackgroundSync)Executes inside loop() only when the UI is sitting at STATE_PATIENT_ID and Wi-Fi is active.Uses an independent non-blocking 4-second timer to avoid busy-waiting.Peeks the oldest JSON in /post_val/, computes $\Delta Z$ against /pre_val/<ID>.csv, and transmits to the cloud.On verified HTTP 200/201, appends to /pre_val/<ID>.csv and deletes the file from /post_val/.Operates completely silently with zero OLED screen redraws or flicker.5. Non-Blocking Timing ArchitectureZero usage of blocking delay() calls throughout the entire sketch.Independent millis() intervals manage keypad debounce (20ms), scale sampling (100ms), ToF ranging (200ms), and network maintenance (10s).
